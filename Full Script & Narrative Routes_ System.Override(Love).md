# Full Narrative Script: System.Override(Love)
## 1. Character Profiles
The Protagonist (Player): A senior student, slightly isolated, tech-savvy. His "Lucidity" stat determines if he can perceive the simulation's flaws.
Iris: The "Crazy Girl."
Reality: A brilliant coder with severe abandonment issues and a history of stalking.
VR: A self-aware admin entity who reshapes her pixels and personality based on the player’s telemetry.
Leo: The Real-World Best Friend.
Reality: A skeptic who is actively investigating Iris.
VR: A hollowed-out shell used by Iris to deliver "safe" dialogue, until the real Leo hacks in.
Maya (Subroutine A): The Sporty Girl. Represents "Vitality."
Chloe (Subroutine B): The Shy Librarian. Represents "Escapism."
## Phase 1: The Real-World Prelude (Days 1–5)
Gameplay focus: Investigation and Suspense. The player can explore their room, the school, and the park.
### Day 1: The Stalker's Shadow
Scene: School Gate, Sunset.
Interaction: If you approach Iris:
Iris: "Oh! [Player Name]! I... I was just waiting for the bus. I know your schedule, you know. 3:15 PM, you leave through the west exit. It's... it's a comfort to know where you are."
Choice:
A) "That's a bit creepy, Iris." (+2 Lucidity)
B) "That's sweet, I guess." (+5 Iris Affection, -2 Lucidity)
### Day 1.5: The SD Card (Critical Investigation)
Scene: Bedroom, Night.
Event: If you shouted at the figure on Day 1, you find an SD card in the grass.
Investigation: Plugging it into your PC reveals a folder: C:/Users/Iris/Archive/My_Future_Husband.
Content: It contains GPS logs of your phone for the last six months, plus a "Soundboard" of your own voice, recorded and spliced into different sentences like "I love you, Iris."
Lucidity Boost: Finding this grants +20 Lucidity. It is required for the True Good Ending.
### Day 2: The Unsolicited Gift
Scene: Classroom, Morning.
Event: You find a lunchbox on your desk. It contains your favorite meal, prepared exactly how you like it.
Interaction: Iris watches you from the doorway, biting her lip.
Choice:
A) Eat it. (+10 Iris Affection, -5 Lucidity).
B) Give it to Leo. (Leo gets sick later; Iris glares at him with pure hatred during lunch).
### Day 3: The Library Standoff
Scene: Library.
Event: You are studying with Chloe. Iris sits at the next table, staring. She isn't reading; she's just watching your hand move as you write.
Interaction: When you leave, you find a note in your textbook: "She's touching the books you touched. Stop talking to her. Only talk to me."
### Day 4: The House Invitation (Branch Point)
Scene: School Hallway.
Iris: "I made something special at my house... Just for us. Will you come over? Please? I don't want to be alone today."
The Choice:
Accept: Triggers the Devoted Reality Route (End of normal gameplay).
Refuse: Triggers the Wary Path (Leads to the Simulation).
### Day 5: The "Accident"
Scene: The Football Field.
Dialogue: Leo is frantic. "I found her 'shrine,' man. We're going to the police after practice."
The Hit: A ball strikes you. Instead of a blackout, the game enters a "Kernel Panic" screen. Text scrolls by: Loading_World_State..., Overwriting_Memory_Sector_0x4F..., User_Status: Captured.
## Phase 2: The Simulation Path (Days 6–10)
Gameplay focus: Social Sim tropes with "Uncanny" glitches.
### Day 6: The "Perfect" Reset
Style: Saturated, vibrant colors. Constant "Lo-Fi" chill beats background music.
Event: You wake up. The atmosphere is bubbly. Maya and Chloe are "Best Friends" now, which contradicts the Real World.
Task: You must complete a "Social Circuit"—talking to everyone to stabilize the world. You notice that every NPC says "The weather is nice" at the exact same second.
### Day 7: The Beach Episode (The Forced Trope)
Scene: The Beach (A clearly looped ocean animation).
The Glitch: The water is solid. You can walk on it.
Iris Interaction: She is in a swimsuit that matches your "Preference" from Day 2.
Event: Iris asks you to put sunscreen on her.
Lucidity Choice: * A) Look at her back. You see a "Product Key" tattooed near her spine. (+15 Lucidity).
B) Ignore the tattoo. (+10 Addiction).
### Day 8: The "Maya" Deletion Sequence
Scene: Gym.
Event: Maya is practicing. Suddenly, she starts "clipping" through the floor.
Maya: "[Player Name]! Help! I can't feel my... legs... I can't feel my... [ERROR: VARIABLE_NOT_FOUND]."
Interaction: Iris walks in, deleting Maya's assets in real-time with a handheld console.
Iris: "She was too loud. You like the quiet, don't you? Now it's just us and the books."
### Day 9: The Ghost in the Machine
Scene: Classroom, Morning.
Event: The simulation begins to break down. The teacher NPC glitches while trying to call Maya's name. A "ghost" basketball bounces down the hallway following Maya's old pathing.
Interaction: Iris panics, manually forcing the NPCs to say Maya "transferred."
Choice:
A) Placate her and pretend everything is fine (+10 Addiction, -5 Lucidity).
B) Taunt her about her broken game (+15 Lucidity, Iris violently deletes the entire school gymnasium).
### Day 10: Chloe's Last Stand
Scene: Library, Afternoon.
Event: Chloe becomes self-aware of her impending deletion. She gives the protagonist a "Corrupted Key_File" (a backdoor diagnostic port that will allow Leo to hack in later).
Interaction: Iris arrives to "defragment" the unused assets.
The Wipe: Iris deletes Chloe and initiates a massive memory wipe of the simulation, erasing the protagonist's memories of the last two days to reset his stress levels. This bridges perfectly into Day 11.
## Phase 3: The Meta-Horror (Days 11–14)
### Day 11: The "Leo" Echoes
Scene: Your Room (Simulation).
Event: Your phone rings. It's Leo.
Dialogue: It's not the "Shell Leo." It's the real one, hacking into the diagnostic channel from Iris's basement.
Real Leo: "I'm in her basement. I found the rig. You're strapped into a medical chair with IV lines in your arm. Don't trust the sunset. The sunset is the trigger."
Follow-up: If you confront Iris immediately, she manually forces a dusk cycle and pushes more sedative into the real-world IV.
### Day 12: The Student Council "Honeypot"
Scene: Student Council Room.
Event: Iris "forgets" her phone on the desk.
The Snoop: If you look, you find a "Live Camera" app. It shows your real body in her basement. Your eyes are rolled back under the visor.
The Encounter: Iris enters, closes the live feed, and turns the scene into a calculated seduction trap. She loosens her collar, invades your space, and tries to make relief, desire, and surrender blur together.
The Trap: If you give in and tell her she's beautiful, you gain +50 Addiction and -30 Lucidity. If you reject the trap, the story moves straight into the final confrontation.
## Phase 4: Final Ending Branches (Full Descriptions)
### 1. "Digital Bliss" (The Eternal Loop - Bad End)
Trigger: Addiction > 70 or choosing "Stay" during the final confrontation.
What Happens: You embrace Iris. She smiles, and the screen glitched violently.
The Outcome: The simulation rewrites itself into a blinding white room where there are no decisions, no interruptions, and no one left except Iris. In the real world, sedative enters the IV and wipes away the last resistant part of you.
The Twist: The final shot is a close-up of the Protagonist's eye in the real world. A needle from the VR rig is seen injecting a blue liquid directly into the tear duct.
### 2. "The Basement Cliffhanger" (The Failed Escape - Neutral End)
Trigger: Lucidity high enough to force a shutdown, but without the Silver Locket paradox route.
What Happens: You reject Iris and force a system crash.
The Outcome: You wake up in the real world. You are strapped to a chair in a room filled with thousands of printed photos of your face. You are too weak to move.
The Encounter: Real-world Iris enters. She is disheveled, holding a kitchen knife and a plate of cold pasta.
Dialogue: "You were so happy in there... Why did you have to break it? Now we have to do this the hard way." She sits on your lap and begins to feed you. The game ends as she whispers, "Don't worry. I'll never let the police find us."
### 3. "Sunrise" (The True Good End)
Requirements: Silver Locket + High Lucidity (>60) + Investigated SD Card.
The Paradox Sequence:
Presentation: You show Iris the Silver Locket.
The Question: "Iris, what is the date inside? If you love me, you'd know my mother's birthday."
The Crash: Iris's AI attempts to search the house for the locket to "read" it, but since you had it in your pocket during the kidnapping, her database is empty.
The Logic Loop: She begins to scream, her character model rapidly switching between Maya, Chloe, and her Real-World self.
The Rescue: The void collapses and Leo reaches you in the real basement as police break in and drag Iris away.
The Epilogue: Six months later. You are at a real cafe with Leo. You aren't dating anyone, and you're in therapy. You see a girl who looks like Iris across the street. You don't panic; you just take a sip of coffee and look at the real sky. It's beautiful.
### 4. "The Glass Coffin" (The Devoted Reality End)
Trigger: Accepted the invitation on Day 4 + High Iris Affection.
What Happens: You go to her house. You see the red flags, but you choose to ignore them because you "want to be loved."
The Outcome: Iris drugs your tea. You wake up in her bedroom, but there is no VR. You are simply locked in.
The Twist: She has cut the tendons in your ankles so you can't run. She treats you like a prince, but you are a pet. The game ends with the two of you watching a movie while your face has already gone blank.
