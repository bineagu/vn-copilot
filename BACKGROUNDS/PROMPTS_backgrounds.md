# AI Image Generation Prompts: Backgrounds

Ordered by first appearance in the SCRIPT folder. Reused backgrounds are listed once. Distinct lighting states, glitch states, simulation states, and endgame voids are kept separate because they would need separate art. Transition-only entries are marked as FX plates so you can decide whether to generate them or do them in-engine.

**Global Background Tag (Append to all prompts):**  
`visual novel background, 2D illustration, anime style, clean line art, cel-shaded, high quality, cinematic composition, subtle psychological horror undertones, liminal atmosphere, absolutely empty, no people, no characters.`
