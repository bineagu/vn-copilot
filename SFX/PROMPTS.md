# AI Sound Generation Prompts: SFX

Ordered by first appearance in the SCRIPT folder. This is a production-facing asset list, not a raw transcript. Repeated or closely related cues are consolidated where a single base sound can be reused with volume, EQ, reverb, or pitch changes in-engine.

**Global SFX Tag (Append to all prompts as needed):**  
`clean isolated sound effect, no music, no voice unless explicitly requested, cinematic, high quality, suitable for psychological horror visual novel, clear transient detail, minimal background noise.`

### Reuse Notes

*   The `dialup` family can cover the major modem-horror cues with filtered variants: `sfx_dialup_screech_static`, `sfx_dialup_phone_burst`, and `sfx_dialup_whine_build`.
*   The hallway and footsteps cues can be reused with reverb changes rather than generating unique files for every walking moment.
*   If you want a tighter first pass, prioritize the stingers and UI cues before the ambient loops.
