# Day 6: The "Perfect" Reset
[BGM: "Virtual Sunshine" - Starts abruptly. Extremely high-energy, bubbly J-Pop with bright synths and an overwhelmingly cheerful beat]
[Background: Classroom, Morning - The colors are intensely saturated. The sky outside the window is a flawless, unnatural shade of cyan.]
[SFX: Cheerful school bell chimes]
Protagonist (Internal):
My eyes snap open.
The sunlight streaming through the window is so bright it almost burns.
Protagonist (Internal):
I'm sitting at my desk. My head feels heavy, packed with cotton. I bring my hand up to my forehead, expecting to feel blood, or a bandage, or... something.
Nothing. My skin is perfectly smooth.
Protagonist:
"Where... the field. I was on the field..."
Protagonist (Internal):
I try to remember yesterday, but it feels like trying to look through a pane of frosted glass. I remember Leo screaming. I remember a sound like a cannon.
But the memory slips away, replaced by a warm, fuzzy feeling. Like pulling a heavy blanket over my head on a cold morning.
[Sprite Enter: Leo - Smiling broadly. His uniform is perfectly clean. His posture is relaxed, completely devoid of the terror from yesterday.]
Leo:
"Morning, sleeping beauty! You were totally out of it. Dreaming about your future wife again?"
Protagonist:
"Leo! The cops, we were supposed to go to—wait. What?"
[Sprite Change: Leo - Tilts his head, looking confused but maintaining a cheerful smile.]
Leo:
"Cops? What are you talking about, man? Did you play too many video games last night? Come on, you need to wake up. The girls are coming over!"
Protagonist (Internal):
The girls? What girls?
Before I can ask, the classroom door slides open.
[Sprite Enter: Maya and Chloe - They enter arm-in-arm. Maya is laughing, and Chloe is smiling brightly, no trace of her usual crippling shyness.]
Protagonist (Internal):
Maya and Chloe.
Wait. They don't know each other. Maya is the track star. Chloe practically lives in the library. They've never spoken a single word to each other in reality.
Maya:
"Hey, [Player Name]! Chloe and I brought some extra snacks. You want some?"
Chloe:
"We made them together this morning! I hope you like them..."
Protagonist (Internal):
They walk over to my desk. They are both looking at me with wide, adoring eyes. It feels incredibly forced. It feels like a script.
And then, she walks in.
[BGM: "Virtual Sunshine" drops the bass, shifting to "Data-Driven Love" - A sweeter, more romantic synth-pop track]
[Sprite Enter: Iris - She looks... different. Her skin has a soft, glowing bloom effect. Her hair is perfectly styled. She looks exactly like the 'type' I described to Leo days ago.]
Protagonist (Internal):
Iris.
My heart stutters. A spike of pure, raw panic tries to pierce through the mental fog, but it’s immediately smothered by a wave of artificial calm.
Iris:
"Good morning, [Player Name]."
Protagonist (Internal):
Her voice is like honey. There is no trace of the obsessive stalker from the library. There is no manic edge. She is the picture-perfect main heroine of a romance game.
Protagonist:
"Iris...?"
[Sprite Change: Iris - Blushes deeply, bringing her hands to her chest]
Iris:
"I'm so glad you're here. The world is just so beautiful today, isn't it? I feel like... like today is the start of something wonderful. Just for us."
Protagonist (Internal):
Maya, Chloe, and Leo all step back slightly, giving Iris the center stage. They don't seem bothered by her intense focus on me. In fact, they look incredibly happy about it.
Like they were programmed to support her.
[SFX: A low, barely audible electronic hum, like a server fan kicking on]
Protagonist (Internal):
I look past Iris. Out the window, I can see the school courtyard. Dozens of students are walking around.
But something is wrong.
Protagonist (Internal):
I watch a group of three students walking. Their footsteps fall at the exact same millisecond.
I look to the back of the classroom. Four students are sitting at their desks.
[Sprite Change: Background NPCs - Their mouths all open simultaneously]
Random Student 1: "The weather is nice today, isn't it?"
Random Student 2: "The weather is nice today, isn't it?"
Random Student 3: "The weather is nice today, isn't it?"
Leo, Maya, and Chloe (In perfect unison): "The weather is nice today, isn't it?"
Protagonist (Internal):
The audio overlaps perfectly. It’s a chorus of identical voice lines.
My blood runs cold. This isn't real. This is a dollhouse. And I am locked inside it.
[Sprite Change: Iris - Her smile falters for a fraction of a second. Her eyes dart to the NPCs, then back to me. The smile returns, a little tighter.]
Iris:
"Don't pay attention to them, [Player Name]. Pay attention to me. What should we do today? We can do anything you want!"
### [CHOICE MENU]
Option A: "Yeah... the weather is nice. Let's hang out." (Give in to the illusion)
Option B: "Leo, why did you say that at the exact same time as them?" (Push back)
### [BRANCH A: Give in to the illusion]
(System: +15 Addiction, -10 Lucidity. You accept the world state.)
Protagonist:
"...Yeah. The weather is nice. Let's just hang out."
[Sprite Change: Iris - Beams with genuine, radiant joy. The ambient lighting in the room literally becomes warmer.]
Iris:
"I knew you'd say that! Let's go to the roof. Maya and Chloe can come too, right girls?"
Maya & Chloe (Unison):
"Of course! We love hanging out with you!"
Protagonist (Internal):
The headache fades away completely. The panic is gone. I don't know how, but this world... it feels good. It feels easy. Nobody is trying to hurt me. Everybody loves me.
Why would I ever want to remember what happened on the football field?
(End Scene - Proceed to Day 7)
### [BRANCH B: Push back]
(System: +15 Lucidity, -5 Addiction. You notice the cracks.)
Protagonist:
"Leo... Maya... why did you all just say the exact same thing at the exact same time?"
[BGM: STOP. Absolute silence for two seconds.]
[Sprite Change: Iris - Her eyes widen. The 'perfect' lighting in the room flickers like a faulty fluorescent bulb.]
Iris:
"They're just... they're just agreeing with each other! Great minds think alike, right?"
Protagonist:
"No, their mouths moved perfectly together. That's not normal. Where are we? Leo, look at me."
[Sprite Change: Leo - His smile remains frozen. His eyes are blank.]
Leo:
"The weather is nice today, isn't it?"
Protagonist (Internal):
I stumble backward, hitting my desk. My heart rate spikes.
[Sprite Change: Iris - Closes the distance between us in a single, impossibly fast frame of animation. She grabs my hands. Her grip is bruising.]
[BGM Play: "Virtual Sunshine" - Resumes, but it is 10% faster and slightly off-pitch]
Iris:
"Don't look at him! Look at me! I worked so hard on this for you! You said you wanted peace! This is peace!"
Protagonist (Internal):
Her voice is echoing, sounding like it's coming from inside my own head.
I pull my hands away. I nod slowly, terrified of what she might do if I reject this entirely.
Protagonist:
"Okay. Okay, Iris. Just... give me some space."
Iris:
"Of course! Anything for you. Let's just have a good day, okay?"
Protagonist (Internal):
She steps back, the perfect smile returning as if nothing happened.
I am trapped. I need to play along. But I can't forget. I have to stay awake.
(End Scene - Proceed to Day 7)
