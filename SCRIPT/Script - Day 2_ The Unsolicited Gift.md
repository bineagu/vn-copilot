# Day 2: The Unsolicited Gift
[BGM: "Gray Suburbia" - Playing slowly, tempo dragged down]
[Background: Classroom, Morning - Overcast, gray light filtering through the windows]
[SFX: Dull murmur of students chatting, distant lockers slamming]
Protagonist (Internal):
My eyes feel like they're full of sand.
Every time I blinked last night, I saw that spreadsheet. The audio clips echoed in the silence of my room.
"...I... love... you... Iris."
Protagonist (Internal):
I walk to my desk at the back of the classroom, moving on autopilot. I keep my head down. If I look at anyone, I might scream. How many of them does she watch? Or is it just me? Am I the only one trapped in whatever game she's playing?
[SFX: Chair sliding out]
Protagonist (Internal):
I drop my bag next to my chair and look down at my desk.
I freeze.
[Sprite Enter: Item - An ornate, perfectly wrapped bento box wrapped in a dark red cloth]
Protagonist (Internal):
Sitting dead center on my desk is a lunchbox.
It’s wrapped meticulously. Tied with a neat little bow. Attached to the top is a small, pale pink sticky note.
[System Graphic: Note - "You were up so late studying! You need your energy. - I <3"]
Protagonist (Internal):
My stomach twists into a tight knot.
She knows I was awake. Of course she does. She's tracking my lights, my phone... God knows what else.
Protagonist:
"Who put this here?"
Protagonist (Internal):
I mutter it under my breath, but my eyes dart to the classroom door.
[Sprite Enter: Iris - Standing in the doorway. She is gripping the doorframe, biting her lower lip, staring intently at my desk.]
Protagonist (Internal):
There she is.
She isn't coming inside. She's just hovering at the threshold, watching my reaction. Her eyes are wide, expectant. Like a dog waiting for a treat.
[Sprite Enter: Leo - Walks up from behind, slapping my shoulder]
[SFX: Loud smack]
Leo:
"Yo! Morning, man. You look like you got run over by a truck."
Protagonist:
"Leo. Hey. I just... didn't sleep well."
[Sprite Change: Leo - Looks down at the desk, eyebrows raised]
Leo:
"Whoa, hello. Did your mom drop off a gourmet lunch or something? That smells incredible."
Protagonist (Internal):
Leo leans in and sniffs the box. I haven't even opened it, but the faint aroma leaking out is unmistakable.
Teriyaki chicken. Extra ginger. A side of tamagoyaki cooked slightly sweet.
My exact favorite meal. Prepared precisely the way I like it. A preference I’ve only ever mentioned in texts to my mom... texts that Iris must have read.
Leo:
"Wait, is there a note? 'From I'? Bro, you have a secret admirer? Open it!"
Protagonist:
"Leo, don't touch it."
Leo:
"Come on, just a peek! I'm starving. I skipped breakfast."
Protagonist (Internal):
I look back at the door. Iris is still there. Her grip on the doorframe is so tight her knuckles are white. If I throw it away, what will she do? If I eat it, am I validating her obsession?
### [CHOICE MENU]
Option A: Eat the lunch.
Option B: Give it to Leo.
### [BRANCH A: Eat the lunch.]
(System: +10 Iris Affection, -5 Lucidity)
Protagonist:
"Leave it, Leo. It's... it's mine."
[Sprite Change: Leo - Puts his hands up in mock surrender, smirking]
Leo:
"Alright, alright! Keep your romantic bento to yourself. Just save me a bite, yeah?"
Protagonist (Internal):
Leo walks over to his desk. With trembling fingers, I untie the red cloth.
I open the lid. It looks like a culinary magazine cover. It’s too perfect.
[SFX: Soft rustling of cloth]
Protagonist (Internal):
I take out the chopsticks. I look toward the door.
Iris is vibrating with excitement. She gives me a tiny, frantic nod.
Protagonist (Internal):
I take a bite.
It’s delicious. It’s exactly how I like it.
And that terrifies me more than if it tasted like poison. A small part of my exhausted brain thinks, 'Well, at least someone cares.' [Sprite Change: Iris - A serene, victorious smile spreads across her face before she turns and disappears down the hall.]
(End Scene - Proceed to Day 3)
### [BRANCH B: Give it to Leo.]
(System: -10 Iris Affection, +10 Lucidity)
Protagonist:
"Actually... you can have it, Leo."
[Sprite Change: Leo - Eyes widen in disbelief]
Leo:
"Wait, seriously? You're giving away premium waifu cooking? Are you sick?"
Protagonist:
"I'm not hungry. My stomach is a mess today. Just... take it to your desk. Please."
Leo:
"Don't mind if I do! You're a lifesaver, man!"
Protagonist (Internal):
Leo happily scoops up the red-wrapped box and heads to his seat in the front row.
I immediately look at the door.
[BGM: STOP]
[BGM Play: "Obsession in D-Minor" - Sudden, sharp cello scrape]
[Sprite Change: Iris - Her expression has completely shattered. Her mouth is slightly open, eyes blown wide in raw shock.]
Protagonist (Internal):
She stares at Leo as he excitedly unwraps the food.
Then, her gaze snaps back to me.
The innocent, expectant look is gone. It is replaced by a look of such absolute, venomous hatred that I actually physically recoil in my chair.
Protagonist (Internal):
It's not directed at me. She looks back at Leo.
If looks could kill, Leo would be bleeding out on the classroom floor.
[Sprite Change: Iris - Turns sharply and vanishes into the hallway]
Protagonist (Internal):
She's gone.
But during lunch period later that day, Leo complains of severe stomach cramps and has to go to the nurse's office. He spends the rest of the day throwing up.
Protagonist (Internal):
She didn't poison it to hurt me.
But she made sure that if anyone else touched what was 'mine', they would regret it.
(End Scene - Proceed to Day 3)
