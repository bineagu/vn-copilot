# The Final Confrontation & Endings
[BGM: STOP. The silence is deafening.]
[Background: A void of raw, scrolling binary code. The Student Council room has completely dissolved.]
Protagonist (Internal):
The simulation is breaking apart.
The walls, the sky, the desks—they've all unraveled into streams of green and black code.
[Sprite Enter: Iris - She is floating slightly above the 'floor'. Her avatar is violently glitching. One moment she looks like Maya, the next Chloe, before snapping back to her original, wide-eyed form.]
Iris (Voice layered with digital distortion):
"You're ruining it! I gave you everything! Why do you keep looking at the outside world?!"
Protagonist:
"Because it's real, Iris! You're keeping me in a cage!"
Iris:
"A cage?! It's a sanctuary! Do you want to go back there? Do you want to go back to the cold? To being a nobody?"
[SFX: A massive, echoing boom, like a server rack crashing to the ground]
Protagonist (Internal):
The void shudders. The real-world hack is working. I can faintly hear police sirens, heavily filtered through layers of static.
Iris hears it too. Panic flashes across her glitching features.
Iris:
"They're at the door. I have to put you to sleep. I have to lock the system."
Protagonist (Internal):
She raises her hand. A massive command prompt appears above her head:
Command: Execute_Neural_Lockdown. Admin_Override_Initiated.
Protagonist (Internal):
This is it. The final moments. I either break the system right now, or I'm trapped in this void forever.

### [ENDING 1: DIGITAL BLISS (Bad End)]
[BGM Play: "Null Pointer Exception" - Ethereal piano that loops endlessly]
Protagonist (Internal):
I look at the command prompt. I look at Iris's desperate, tear-filled eyes.
My body feels so heavy. The memory of the Honeypot is still burning in my mind—that intoxicating, paralyzing warmth.
Why am I fighting? Why am I trying to go back to a world where I was miserable?
Protagonist:
"Wait. Iris, stop."
[Sprite Change: Iris - Lowers her hand slightly, the red command prompt pausing.]
Protagonist:
"I'm tired of fighting. I'll stay. I want to stay with you."
[Sprite Change: Iris - The glitches stop immediately. Her avatar stabilizes into her absolute 'perfect' form. She floats down to me, wrapping her arms around my neck.]
Iris:
"I knew it. I knew you'd see it my way. We're going to be so happy, [Player Name]."
Protagonist (Internal):
As she kisses me, the sirens fade away. The void begins to rewrite itself, building a pristine, blindingly white room around us.
But the feeling of her lips is suddenly accompanied by a sharp, icy prick in my real-world arm.
[System Graphic: CG Unlock - A close-up of the Protagonist's physical eye in the real world. A tear rolls down his cheek, illuminated by the harsh blue light of the VR rig. In the reflection of the visor lens, a mechanical syringe is plunging blue liquid into his IV line.]
Protagonist (Internal):
The sedative hits my system.
My mind goes entirely blank. There is no Leo. There is no school. There is only Iris. Forever.
[SYSTEM TEXT: ENDING 1 - DIGITAL BLISS]
(Fade to black. Return to Main Menu. Note: The Main Menu UI is now permanently altered, featuring only Iris.)
### [ENDING 2: THE BASEMENT CLIFFHANGER (Neutral End)]
[BGM Play: "System Crash" - Blaring violently]
Protagonist:
"No! I'm not letting you do this!"
Protagonist (Internal):
I lunge forward, grabbing her arm before she can execute the command. I don't know how VR physics work, but I pour every ounce of my willpower into rejecting her control.
Iris:
"Let go of me! [ERR_ACCESS_DENIED]!"
Protagonist (Internal):
The conflict between my high Lucidity and her Admin status causes a massive logic loop. The code around us turns a violent, blistering red.
Protagonist:
"SYSTEM SHUTDOWN! WAKE UP! WAKE UP!"
[SFX: An ear-splitting shatter of digital glass]
[BGM: STOP. Absolute, dead silence.]
[Background: Total Blackness]
Protagonist (Internal):
I gasp, my lungs burning as they pull in real, stale air.
The VR rig over my eyes is dead. I'm in darkness. I can smell mold, copper, and old carpet.
Protagonist (Internal):
I try to move my arms, but heavy leather straps bite into my wrists. I'm strapped to the medical chair. My muscles have atrophied; I can barely lift my head.
[SFX: The heavy thud of footsteps on wooden stairs directly above me]
Protagonist (Internal):
The cops didn't make it in time.
The basement door creaks open at the top of the stairs. A sliver of pale yellow light cuts through the darkness.
[Sprite Enter: Iris (Real World) - She is disheveled, wearing a stained hoodie. Her eyes are sunken, but her smile is wide and terrifying. She is holding a large kitchen knife in one hand and a plate of cold pasta in the other.]
Iris (Voice cracking, real and unpolished):
"You broke my world, honey... I spent so long coding it for you."
Protagonist:
"Iris... don't..."
[Sprite Change: Iris - Slowly descends the stairs, the knife glinting in the faint light.]
Iris:
"If you won't love me in there... you'll just have to stay down here until you learn. Don't worry. I'll never let them find us. Now... open wide. It's time to eat."
Protagonist (Internal):
She sits on my lap, raising the fork to my mouth. I close my eyes. The nightmare has only just begun.
[SYSTEM TEXT: ENDING 2 - THE BASEMENT CLIFFHANGER]
(Fade to black. Return to Main Menu.)
### [ENDING 3: SUNRISE (The True Good End)]
[BGM: Silence]
Protagonist:
"Iris. Look at me."
[Sprite Change: Iris - Pauses, her hand hovering over the command prompt.]
Protagonist (Internal):
I reach into my pocket. My digital hand closes around an object that feels incredibly heavy. It’s rendering poorly, covered in gray static.
Protagonist:
"This simulation is built on my memories, right? Everything in here is generated from the data you stole from my phone and my schedule."
Iris:
"Yes! And I made it perfect!"
Protagonist:
"Then tell me what this is."
[System Graphic: Item - The Silver Locket appears on screen, glitching heavily]
Protagonist (Internal):
I hold out the Silver Locket I found in the old park.
[Sprite Change: Iris - Her avatar leans in, her eyes scanning it. A look of genuine confusion crosses her face.]
Iris:
"It's... a necklace. I rendered it for you. It's our symbol!"
Protagonist:
"What's the date engraved on the inside, Iris?"
Iris:
"It... it says..."
[SFX: A high-pitched, whining dial-up tone begins to build]
Protagonist (Internal):
She tries to read it. Her AI searches her vast database of my life, but it hits a wall. She didn't know I found it. She didn't know I had it in my pocket when she hit me with the football. It isn't in her code.
Iris (Voice glitching rapidly):
"It says... 'Always... Yours'...? No... [DATA_NOT_FOUND]. [INDEX_ERROR]."
Protagonist:
"It’s my mother’s birthday. March 12th. You don't know me, Iris. You only love a save file."
[BGM Play: "The First Real Breath" - Warm, swelling orchestral music begins to build]
[SFX: A massive explosion of static]
Protagonist (Internal):
The logic paradox hits her core programming like a sledgehammer.
She screams—a horrifying, dual-toned shriek of a machine and a broken girl. Her avatar rapidly switches between Maya, Chloe, and herself before violently shattering into thousands of polygons.
[Background: The void collapses into pure, blinding white light]
[SFX: Wood splintering, heavy boots hitting concrete, loud voices shouting "POLICE! SECURE THE ROOM!"]
Protagonist (Internal):
The light fades.
I feel a rough hand tear the VR visor off my face.
The sudden rush of cold air makes me gasp. I blink against the harsh glare of police flashlights cutting through the dark basement.
Leo (Voice breaking with relief):
"I got you! Medics, get in here! We got him!"
Protagonist (Internal):
Leo is standing over me, looking older, exhausted, and real. In the background, I can hear Iris sobbing hysterically as two officers handcuff her against the wall.
I close my eyes, and for the first time in weeks, I let myself fall asleep.
[Background: A sunlit, real-world Cafe. Six months later.]
Protagonist (Internal):
The coffee here is bitter.
I like it that way. It tastes real.
[Sprite Enter: Leo - Wearing casual clothes, holding two cups of coffee. He sits across from me.]
Leo:
"Hey man. How was the physical therapy session today?"
Protagonist:
"Getting better. I walked a full mile without the cane."
Leo:
"That's awesome. You'll be back on the track team by spring."
Protagonist (Internal):
I take a sip of the coffee and look out the window.
Across the street, a girl with short hair is walking a dog. For a split second, my heart skips a beat. I think I see her. I think I see Iris.
But I blink, and it's just a stranger. Just a girl living her own life.
Protagonist:
"Leo?"
Leo:
"Yeah?"
Protagonist:
"The sun feels... actually warm today."
[Sprite Change: Leo - Smiles warmly, a genuine, unscripted smile.]
Leo:
"Yeah. It does."
[System Graphic: The camera pans up to the clear blue, completely un-pixelated sky.]
[SYSTEM TEXT: ENDING 3 - SUNRISE]
