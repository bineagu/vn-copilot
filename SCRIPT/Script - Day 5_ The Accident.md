# Day 5: The "Accident"
[BGM: "Gray Suburbia" - Playing, but the tempo is noticeably rushed and erratic]
[Background: School Football Field, Late Afternoon - The sky is bruised with heavy, gray clouds]
[SFX: Distant shouts of the track team, the rhythmic thud of a football being kicked]
Protagonist (Internal):
I survived the night.
I didn't sleep a wink. I just sat in the corner of my room with a baseball bat, listening to the wind rattle the windowpanes. But the sun came up, and she didn't break in.
I made it to school. I avoided the hallways. I hid in the boys' bathroom during lunch.
Protagonist (Internal):
Now, school is over. I'm standing at the edge of the football field, waiting for Leo to finish practice.
We're going to the police. I have the SD card in my pocket. I don't care if they think I'm crazy. I can't live like this anymore.
[SFX: Rapid, heavy footsteps approaching on the gravel track]
[Sprite Enter: Leo - Wearing his practice uniform, sweating profusely. He looks absolutely terrified, completely out of breath.]
Leo:
"[Player Name]! Hey! Man, I'm so glad you're still here."
Protagonist:
"Leo? What's wrong? You look like you just saw a ghost."
[Sprite Change: Leo - Grabs my shoulders, his grip painfully tight. He looks around frantically.]
Leo:
"Worse. Man, it's so much worse than we thought."
Protagonist:
"What did you do? Did you talk to her?"
Leo:
"No. I skipped fifth period. I... I broke into her backyard. Her parents are never home, right? I looked through the basement window."
Protagonist (Internal):
My stomach drops into my shoes.
Leo:
"She has a shrine, man. It's not just photos. She has... pieces of your hair. Clothes you threw away. And there's this massive machine down there. It looks like a medical chair covered in wires and server racks."
[BGM Play: "Obsession in D-Minor" - Swells loudly, the cello drone vibrating aggressively]
Leo:
"We have to go to the cops right now. My car is in the lot. Come on."
Protagonist:
"Okay. Okay, let's go. We have the SD card, we have your testimony—"
Protagonist (Internal):
I turn to follow him toward the parking lot.
As I sweep my gaze across the bleachers, my breath catches in my throat.
[Sprite Enter: Iris - Standing at the very top of the bleachers. She is too far away to see her expression clearly, but she is pointing her index finger directly at me.]
Protagonist:
"Leo. Look."
[Sprite Change: Leo - Turns to look at the bleachers. He pales.]
Leo:
"Don't look at her. Just walk. Keep walking!"
Protagonist (Internal):
Iris lowers her hand. She doesn't run toward us. She just mimics the motion of kicking something.
[SFX: An impossibly loud, booming THWACK, like a cannon firing, overlapping with the sound of a leather football being kicked.]
Protagonist (Internal):
I hear the sound before I see it.
A stray football from the practice field. It's moving at a speed that defies physics. It isn't an arc. It's a straight, lethal line.
Directly at my head.
Leo:
"WATCH OU—!"
[SFX: A sickening crunch]
[BGM: STOP. Absolute, dead silence.]
[Background: The screen freezes completely. No fade to black. The colors become painfully sharp and over-saturated.]
Protagonist (Internal):
Pain explodes behind my eyes.
But I don't fall.
The ground doesn't rush up to meet me. Gravity just... stops working.
[Sprite Change: Leo - Frozen mid-shout. His face is distorted, pixelated at the edges like a corrupted JPEG.]
Protagonist (Internal):
Leo isn't moving. The football is hovering six inches from my face, frozen in mid-air. The clouds above have stopped drifting.
Protagonist:
"Leo...? What's... happening?"
Protagonist (Internal):
My voice doesn't make a sound. My mouth moves, but there is no audio.
[SFX: A loud, harsh digital dial-up screech tears through the speakers, followed by heavy static]
Protagonist (Internal):
The world begins to tear apart. The sky fractures into jagged black lines. The grass beneath my feet turns into a sea of scrolling green binary code.
I try to scream, but my throat is filled with white noise.
[System Graphic: The screen goes completely black. A harsh, white command prompt appears in the top left corner.]
KERNEL PANIC: UNEXPECTED KINETIC TRAUMA
Initiating Emergency Deep-Dive Protocol...
Establishing Neural Link... [OK]
Suppressing Real_World_Memory_Sector_0x4F... [OK]
Loading_World_State: 'OUR_PARADISE.exe'...
Protagonist (Internal):
I can feel something cold and metallic sliding over my eyes. I can feel a needle piercing the skin of my neck.
But it feels so far away. The pain from the football fades into a warm, buzzing numbness.
User_Status: Captured.
Welcome Home, [Player Name].
[SFX: A cheerful, bright digital chime, like a console booting up]
[System Graphic: The command prompt vanishes in a flash of blinding white light]
(End Scene - Proceed to Day 6: The "Perfect" Reset)
