# Day 11: The "Leo" Echoes
[BGM: STOP. The scene begins in total, oppressive silence.]
[Background: Protagonist's Bedroom (Simulation), Afternoon - Everything is perfectly neat, completely devoid of the clutter from the real world.]
Protagonist (Internal):
Days nine and ten didn't happen.
At least, I don't remember them. The simulation just... skipped. One moment I was running from the gym, and the next, I was waking up in my bed.
Protagonist (Internal):
I haven't left my room. I don't want to see what the school looks like without Maya. I don't want to see Chloe, waiting to be erased.
Most of all, I don't want to see her.
[SFX: A sharp, sudden ringing sound. It isn't the cheerful digital chime of the VR interface. It’s the harsh, vibrating ringtone of my real-world phone.]
Protagonist (Internal):
I flinch, falling backward off the bed.
The sound is coming from the VR smartphone sitting on my desk.
[System Graphic: Phone Screen - Caller ID reads: ERR_UNKNOWN_INTRUSION]
Protagonist (Internal):
I slowly reach out and pick it up. My hands are shaking. I press 'Accept'.
Protagonist:
"...Hello?"
[SFX: Heavy, crackling static, followed by frantic, heavy breathing]
Leo (Voice Over - Highly distorted, clipping heavily):
"[Player Name]?! ...hear me?! Is this... working? Command line accepted... come on, man, answer me!"
Protagonist (Internal):
My heart stops.
Protagonist:
"Leo?! Leo, is that you? The real you?!"
Leo (Voice Over):
"Oh my god. You're alive. Look, I don't have much time. I brute-forced my way into the audio channel through the rig's diagnostic port."
Protagonist:
"The rig? Leo, where are you?!"
[BGM Play: "Basement Heartbeat" - A heavy, rhythmic industrial thumping begins, simulating a racing heart]
Leo (Voice Over):
"I'm in her basement. Man... it's a nightmare down here. You're strapped to this... medical chair. Your eyes are rolled back. She's got IV drips hooked up to your arm."
Protagonist (Internal):
I look down at my virtual arm. It looks perfect and healthy. But a phantom prick of pain suddenly echoes in my real-world vein. I feel violently sick.
Protagonist:
"You have to get me out! Pull the headset off!"
Leo (Voice Over):
"I can't! The terminal says if I force-disconnect the neural link without a shutdown sequence, it could fry your frontal lobe! I already called the cops. They're on their way. But she's..."
[SFX: A loud, muffled bang on the other end of the line, like a door slamming upstairs in the real world]
Leo (Voice Over):
"Shit. She's back. She wasn't supposed to be back from the store yet."
Protagonist:
"Leo! Get out of there! She's crazy!"
Leo (Voice Over):
"Listen to me! I'm going to hide until the cops get here. But you have to stay awake in there! Whatever she does, do not trust the sunset!"
Protagonist:
"The sunset? What do you mean?!"
Leo (Voice Over):
"The sunset in the game! It's a synchronization trigger! When the sky turns red in there, she's injecting you with a sedative in here to put you into a deeper sleep cycle! If she triggers it manually, you might not wake up before—"
[SFX: A piercing, deafening dial-up screech bursts through the phone speaker]
Protagonist (Internal):
I drop the phone, clutching my ears. The screen cracks, bleeding black pixels across the digital glass. The call is dead.
[BGM: STOP]
Protagonist (Internal):
Silence slams back into the room.
He found me. I'm strapped to a chair. The cops are coming.
I just have to survive until they get here. I just have to stop her from triggering the sunset.
[SFX: Three slow, deliberate knocks on my bedroom door]
Protagonist (Internal):
Every muscle in my body locks up.
Iris (From behind the door - Voice perfectly sweet, but tightly strained):
"Honey? Who were you talking to just now? I thought I heard another voice in your room."
Protagonist (Internal):
She knows. She felt the intrusion in the system. The door handle begins to slowly turn.
### [CHOICE MENU]
Option A: "Nobody. I was just talking to myself." (Lie and hide the truth)
Option B: "Leo is in your basement. It's over, Iris." (Confront her)
### [BRANCH A: "Nobody. I was just talking to myself."]
(System: +15 Lucidity. You buy time.)
Protagonist:
"Nobody! I was... I was just talking to myself. Having a weird dream."
[Sprite Enter: Iris - Opens the door and steps inside. Her eyes are darting rapidly around the room, scanning the code.]
Iris:
"A dream? That's strange. The firewall flagged an external ping. Are you sure you're feeling okay, [Player Name]?"
Protagonist:
"Yeah. My head just hurts."
[Sprite Change: Iris - Her eyes lock onto the shattered phone on the floor. She smiles, but it's utterly terrifying.]
Iris:
"Oh, you dropped your phone. Clumsy. Don't worry, I'll delete that item and spawn you a new one. Let's go to the Student Council room, okay? I have a surprise for you."
Protagonist (Internal):
She knows I'm lying. But she's pretending not to, trying to keep the simulation stable. I nod slowly. I have to play along until Leo shuts her down.
(End Scene - Proceed to Day 12)
### [BRANCH B: "Leo is in your basement. It's over."]
(System: -20 Lucidity, +20 Addiction. Iris forces a system correction.)
Protagonist:
"Leo is in your basement. The police are on their way. It's over, Iris!"
[Sprite Enter: Iris - The door bursts open. She isn't smiling. Her face is a mask of pure, unadulterated fury. The walls of the bedroom instantly turn a deep, violent crimson.]
[BGM Play: "System Crash" - Heavily distorted, blaring loudly]
Iris:
"HOW DARE HE TOUCH MY THINGS!"
Protagonist (Internal):
She doesn't walk toward me. She just raises her hand.
Iris:
"Command: Override_User_Lucidity. Force_Time_Cycle: DUSK."
Protagonist:
"No! Wait!"
[Background: The lighting in the room shifts instantly from afternoon cyan to a deep, bloody orange sunset.]
Protagonist (Internal):
The sunset. The trigger.
In the real world, the needle is going into my arm. In the VR, my knees buckle instantly. The room spins out of control.
[Sprite Change: Iris - Crouches over me as I fall, her face blurring as my vision fails.]
Iris:
"You're going to sleep now. And when you wake up, that boy will be gone, and we will finally be alone."
Protagonist (Internal):
The sedative pulls me under. The last thing I hear is the sound of sirens in the real world, fading into the digital static.
(End Scene - Proceed to Day 12)
