# Day 7: The Beach Episode
[BGM: "Virtual Sunshine" - Playing brightly, but the volume occasionally dips and swells like a poor connection]
[Background: A pristine, blindingly white beach. The ocean is a perfect, gradient cyan.]
[SFX: Seagull crying (Audio file repeats exactly every 4 seconds)]
[SFX: Waves crashing (Audio file repeats exactly every 6 seconds)]
Protagonist (Internal):
Summer vacation.
I don't remember the semester ending. I don't remember taking my exams. But when I woke up this morning, Leo texted me saying we were all going to the beach.
Protagonist (Internal):
I'm standing on the sand. It doesn't yield beneath my sandals. It feels like walking on a textured plastic floor.
I look out at the ocean. The waves roll in, crash, and pull back. Over and over.
It’s a perfect loop.
Protagonist (Internal):
I walk down to the shoreline. I want to feel the water. I want to feel something cold and real.
I step forward, bracing for the chill of the ocean.
[SFX: A dull thud as my foot strikes the surface]
Protagonist (Internal):
My foot doesn't sink.
I'm standing on top of the water. It's completely solid. Like a sheet of painted glass. I look down and see the foam of the wave trapped beneath the sole of my shoe.
[Sprite Enter: Iris - Wearing a swimsuit. She comes running down the beach, waving her arms.]
Protagonist (Internal):
I quickly step backward onto the "sand" before she gets too close.
Iris:
"[Player Name]! The water looks so beautiful today, doesn't it?"
Protagonist (Internal):
I stare at her. Her swimsuit... it's exactly the style I told Leo I liked weeks ago. Down to the specific color.
She did this. She rendered this entire place just to play out a fantasy.
Protagonist:
"Yeah. It's... it's very still."
[Sprite Change: Iris - Pouts playfully, putting her hands on her hips]
Iris:
"You're so serious! You need to relax. We're supposed to be having fun! Oh, actually..."
[SFX: Plastic bottle squeezing]
[Sprite Change: Iris - Holds up a bottle of sunscreen. She smiles, a faint blush dusting her cheeks.]
Iris:
"I can't quite reach my back. Would you mind helping me? I don't want to get a sunburn."
Protagonist (Internal):
A sunburn. In a world with a painted sky.
But she's looking at me with those huge, expectant eyes. If I refuse, she might realize I'm not playing along.
Protagonist:
"Sure. Turn around."
Protagonist (Internal):
She turns her back to me, sweeping her hair over her shoulder.
I pour a drop of the sunscreen into my palm. It smells like coconut, but underneath that, there's a sharp, ozone smell. Like an overheating computer tower.
Protagonist (Internal):
I raise my hand to her back.
As I get closer, my eyes adjust to the glare of the artificial sun on her skin. There is something just below her left shoulder blade.
It looks like a shadow at first. But the edges are too sharp.
### [CHOICE MENU]
Option A: Look closely at the mark on her back.
Option B: Ignore it and just apply the sunscreen. Keep her happy.
### [BRANCH A: Look closely at the mark.]
(System: +15 Lucidity, -10 Addiction. You see the code.)
Protagonist (Internal):
I lean in, squinting against the bright light.
It isn't a shadow. It isn't a birthmark.
It’s text. Etched directly into the texture of her skin model, in tiny, rigid black font.
[System Graphic: Close up image - Black text on pale skin: Product_Key: IRIS_SYS_V2.4_ADMIN]
Protagonist (Internal):
A product key.
She isn't even trying to hide what she is anymore. Or maybe she just didn't expect me to look this closely.
[Sprite Change: Iris - Suddenly jerks away, spinning around to face me. Her eyes are wide, and the edges of her hair are heavily pixelated.]
[BGM: Music stutters, skipping a beat]
Iris:
"What are you looking at?!"
Protagonist:
"Nothing! I was just... checking for sand."
[Sprite Change: Iris - The pixelation smooths out. Her smile returns, but it's strained. Her voice sounds slightly metallic.]
Iris:
"Just put the lotion on, [Player Name]. Please. Don't look too hard at things. It ruins the magic."
Protagonist (Internal):
I silently apply the sunscreen. My hands are shaking.
V2.4. How many versions of her have there been? How many times has she reset this world?
I need to find a way out. Soon.
(End Scene - Proceed to Day 8)
### [BRANCH B: Ignore it and apply the sunscreen.]
(System: +10 Addiction, -5 Lucidity. You embrace the fantasy.)
Protagonist (Internal):
I shake my head and look away.
It doesn't matter what the shadow is. A glitch, a birthmark, whatever.
I place my hands on her back and gently rub the sunscreen in.
[Sprite Change: Iris - Let's out a soft, contented sigh. She leans back slightly into my touch.]
Iris:
"Mmm... thank you. Your hands are so warm."
Protagonist (Internal):
She feels real. Her skin is warm. The fake coconut smell fills my lungs, and the looping sound of the waves suddenly feels hypnotic rather than terrifying.
I close my eyes for a second.
Protagonist (Internal):
If I just stop questioning it... this isn't so bad.
She loves me. She gave me a perfect summer day. I don't have to worry about school, or my parents, or the future. I just have to be here with her.
[Sprite Change: Iris - Turns her head to look at me over her shoulder. Her smile is completely triumphant.]
Iris:
"I'm so glad we're together, [Player Name]. I promise... every day will be exactly like this. Forever."
Protagonist (Internal):
I smile back.
Deep down, a tiny voice is screaming. But the sun is so bright, and the screaming is so quiet.
(End Scene - Proceed to Day 8)
