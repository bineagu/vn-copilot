# Day 10: Chloe's Last Stand
[BGM: "Data-Driven Love" - Playing very slowly, distorted with heavy reverb]
[Background: Library, Afternoon - The bookshelves are glitching. Some books are floating inches above the wood, while others clip through each other.]
[SFX: The hum of the air conditioner is deafeningly loud, drowning out the ambient silence]
Protagonist (Internal):
The simulation is dying.
Since Iris threw her tantrum yesterday, the world has been falling apart. Doors lead to nowhere. NPC pathing is completely broken; students are just walking into walls, their feet sliding on the floor.
Protagonist (Internal):
I'm in the library. I came here to hide, hoping the quiet would help me think of an escape plan.
But I'm not alone.
[Sprite Enter: Chloe - She is hiding under a table in the corner. Her sprite is shaking violently. Half of her face is stuck in a low-resolution texture.]
Chloe:
"P-P-Please... I don't... I don't want to forget."
Protagonist:
"Chloe?"
[Sprite Change: Chloe - Looks up. Her text box is glitching, filling with random symbols before the text actually appears.]
Chloe:
"$&@! [Player Name]... The books. The stories. She's burning them all. The memory... the RAM... it's all being reallocated to HER."
Protagonist (Internal):
I kneel down next to the table. Chloe isn't acting like a shy anime girl with a crush. She's acting like a sentient program realizing it's about to be formatted.
Protagonist:
"Chloe, what is she doing? Can you help me log out?"
Chloe:
"No... no logout. Admin locked the ports. But... but I found something in the archive before she corrupted it."
[Sprite Change: Chloe - Reaches into her cardigan and pulls out a glowing, heavily pixelated square. It looks like a corrupted save file.]
Chloe:
"It's a backdoor. A diagnostic port. I can't use it, but... if a connection comes from the outside... this will keep the port open. Take it."
[System Graphic: Item Received - "Corrupted Key_File"]
Protagonist (Internal):
I take the glowing square. It sinks into my virtual hand.
(System: Secret Item Acquired. This explains how Leo is able to hack the comms channel on Day 11).
[SFX: A heavy, slow footstep at the library entrance]
[BGM: STOP]
[Sprite Enter: Iris - She floats slightly above the ground. Her eyes are glowing with a harsh blue light. She looks more like a machine than a girl.]
Iris:
"Chloe. I told you it was time for defragmentation."
[Sprite Change: Chloe - Screams, backing further under the table.]
Chloe:
"No! Please! I have feelings! I like the quiet! I like the books! Don't turn me into nothing!"
Iris:
"You are just unused assets. [Player Name] doesn't need you. He only needs me."
Protagonist:
"Iris, stop! Leave her alone!"
[Sprite Change: Iris - Turns her head toward me. Her neck cracks loudly, unnatural and robotic.]
Iris:
"You're stressed again. The anomalies are causing your heart rate to rise. I need to clean the system. For your own good."
Protagonist (Internal):
She raises her hand.
### [CHOICE MENU]
Option A: Try to pull Chloe away from her.
Option B: Brace yourself for the deletion.
### [BRANCH A / B: The outcome is identical, highlighting the player's helplessness]
Protagonist:
"Don't do it!"
[SFX: A massive, blinding flash of white light, accompanied by a deafening digital screech]
[Background: Total White Void]
Protagonist (Internal):
The world goes entirely white. The library, the books, Chloe—they are instantly vaporized.
I fall to my knees in the empty, blank space.
Iris (Voice booming from everywhere at once):
"System overload. Too many errors. Initiating deep memory wipe. Don't worry, [Player Name]. When you wake up, it will be just you and me. Perfect. Clean. Forever."
Protagonist (Internal):
The white void rushes into my eyes. My memories of the last two days begin to shatter like glass. I remember Maya falling. I remember Chloe hiding. And then... the memories just dissolve into static.
[System Graphic: Overwriting_Memory_Sector... Rebooting Day Cycle...]
(End Scene - The game skips forward. Proceed directly to Day 11: The "Leo" Echoes).
