# Day 9: The Ghost in the Machine
[BGM: "Virtual Sunshine" - Playing, but the audio track frequently drops out completely for 2-3 seconds at a time]
[Background: Classroom, Morning - The lighting is erratic. Sometimes bright, sometimes dimming like a brownout.]
[SFX: School bell chimes, but cuts off abruptly halfway through]
Protagonist (Internal):
I didn't sleep. I just sat on the edge of my virtual bed, waiting for the floor to open up and swallow me.
But morning came anyway. The simulation forced the cycle to continue.
Protagonist (Internal):
I'm sitting at my desk. I look at the spot where Maya used to sit.
The desk is gone. It didn't just disappear; the floorboards underneath it are perfectly fused together, as if a desk had never been there in the history of the school.
[Sprite Enter: Generic Teacher NPC - Standing at the chalkboard]
Teacher NPC:
"Good morning, class. Let us begin roll call.
Akira... Present.
Chloe... Present."
[Sprite Enter: Chloe - Sitting across the room. She looks absolutely terrified, trembling and clutching her notebook.]
Teacher NPC:
"Maya... Maya..."
Protagonist (Internal):
The Teacher NPC freezes. His mouth stays open. The AI script is looking for a character asset that Iris permanently deleted.
Teacher NPC (Audio skipping):
"Ma-Ma-Ma-Ma-Ma-Maya..."
[SFX: A high-pitched error tone]
[Sprite Enter: Iris - She hurries to the front of the classroom, her tablet in hand. She looks stressed, swiping rapidly on the screen.]
Iris:
"She transferred! Maya transferred schools yesterday! Right, class?"
[Sprite Change: Background NPCs - All snap their heads toward Iris simultaneously]
Random Students (Unison):
"Maya transferred schools yesterday. We wish her the best."
Teacher NPC:
"Maya transferred. Moving on to the lesson."
Protagonist (Internal):
The teacher turns back to the chalkboard and begins writing in invisible chalk.
Iris lets out a heavy sigh, wiping a bead of digital sweat from her forehead. She turns and looks at me, instantly replacing her stressed frown with a blinding, fake smile.
[Sprite Change: Iris - Walks over to my desk, leaning down]
Iris:
"Good morning, honey! Isn't it a beautifully quiet day? It's so much more peaceful without all that running around."
Protagonist:
"Iris... the teacher just broke. Everyone is acting like robots."
[Sprite Change: Iris - Her smile tightens. The room dims slightly.]
Iris:
"They're just adjusting to the patch update. I'm streamlining the memory usage. You don't need them anyway. You only need me. We're going to have a perfect day today."
[SFX: A loud, echoing THUD from the hallway]
Protagonist (Internal):
Iris flinches.
I look out the open classroom door.
[Background: Hallway - A basketball is bouncing down the empty corridor. But nobody is dribbling it.]
Protagonist (Internal):
The physics engine is still running Maya's daily routine, even though her character model is gone. The ball bounces down the hall, guided by an invisible ghost.
[Sprite Change: Iris - Slams her hands down on my desk. Her eyes are wide and panicked.]
Iris:
"Don't look at that! Look at me! I said look at me!"
### [CHOICE MENU]
Option A: "Okay! I'm looking at you. Just calm down." (Placate her)
Option B: "You can't even control your own game, can you?" (Taunt her)
### [BRANCH A: Placate her]
(System: +10 Addiction, -5 Lucidity)
Protagonist:
"Okay, Iris. I'm looking. I'm right here."
[Sprite Change: Iris - Let's out a shaky breath. The room brightens back to its normal, saturated colors.]
Iris:
"Thank you. Thank you. I just... I want it to be perfect for you. I'll clean up the leftover code tonight. Just stay with me, okay?"
Protagonist (Internal):
She's losing her grip on the world. The cracks are showing everywhere. But as long as I play along, she doesn't hurt me. I nod slowly.
(End Scene - Proceed to Day 10)
### [BRANCH B: Taunt her]
(System: +15 Lucidity, Iris Stability drops)
Protagonist:
"You deleted her, but you couldn't even delete her right. You can't even control your own game."
[Sprite Change: Iris - Steps back as if physically struck. The shadows in the classroom lengthen aggressively.]
[BGM Play: "System Crash" - Short, violent burst of noise]
Iris:
"I control EVERYTHING! I am a god in here! You don't know how hard it is to maintain a cohesive reality matrix for an ungrateful user!"
Protagonist (Internal):
She pulls up her tablet, her fingers flying across the screen in a blur of rage.
Iris:
"Fine! You want it clean? I'll purge the entire athletic module!"
[SFX: The sound of rushing wind. Out the window, the entire school gymnasium simply vanishes, replaced by a wall of white void.]
Protagonist (Internal):
I stare in horror as a chunk of the world is just... gone. She storms out of the classroom, leaving me in the broken, glitching room.
(End Scene - Proceed to Day 10)
